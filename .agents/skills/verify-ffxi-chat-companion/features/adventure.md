# Adventure readout

## Sub-features

Read the sanitized status, current zone and reason.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Send `!status`, `!zone`, or `!why` in live chat.

## Driving it with the offline verifier

Run Drive. Verify the three paired replies: the fixture objective and healthy state with Huge Hornet observation, South Gustaberg (ID 107), and the bounded-safety-check reason.

## Gotchas

The batch is seeded with fresh status. Missing and stale status are not covered by this journey; do not treat a fixture result as access to real game state.
